# Participation Grade Book

A teacher tracks student participation in one or more classes through grade books.

## Language

**Grade book**:
The participation record for one class, including its roster, behaviors, and student grades.
_Avoid_: Sheet, spreadsheet, class

**Local grade book**:
A grade book whose current data is kept in this browser without a linked Google Sheet.
_Avoid_: Unsaved grade book

**Synced grade book**:
A grade book linked to a readable Google Sheet while retaining a local copy for use when Google is unavailable.
_Avoid_: Google grade book

**Roster**:
The students whose participation is tracked in a grade book.
_Avoid_: Class list

**Behavior**:
A teacher-defined dimension of participation graded Yes or No within one grade book, identified by a name and emoji.
_Avoid_: Category, dimension, criterion

**Archived behavior**:
A behavior no longer used for dates after its archive date but retained in past grades.
_Avoid_: Deleted behavior

**Daily grade**:
A Yes or No judgment for one student, date, and behavior.
_Avoid_: Score, mark

**Local backup**:
A downloadable copy of browser-stored grade book data that can be restored into the app.
_Avoid_: Sync copy
