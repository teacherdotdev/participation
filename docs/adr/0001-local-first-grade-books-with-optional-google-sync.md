# Local-first grade books with optional Google sync

Teachers can create and use multiple grade books without a Google account; each grade book has its own roster and editable Yes/No behaviors. A first-time teacher enters a roster, reviews the default behaviors, and can then grade without authorization. Teachers choose which local grade books to connect; connecting one creates a readable Google Sheet containing its existing data, and the app remains the supported editing path. Connected grade books synchronize on open and after each save, and keep accepting local edits while Google is unavailable for later synchronization. Signing out or disconnecting Google leaves local grade books and existing Google Sheets intact. This reverses the original Google-first setup to let teachers begin grading before authorization, while retaining a portable, readable remote copy. Roster and grade data stay between the browser and the teacher's Google Sheet. (Authorization is browser-only; see ADR 0004.)
