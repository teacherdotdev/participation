# Behavior changes preserve history

Grade books start with five editable Yes/No behaviors. Renaming a behavior or changing its emoji updates how both current and past grades are labeled; removing it archives the behavior so past grades remain intact while future dates no longer use it. A newly added behavior applies only from its addition date, and at least one behavior must remain active. This preserves historical grading records and their denominators rather than fabricating grades for dates before a behavior existed or erasing grades after removal.
